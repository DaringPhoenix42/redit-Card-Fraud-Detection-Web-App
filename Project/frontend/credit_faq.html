Credit Card FAQs

Find answers to common questions about credit cards, security, and best practices for safe usage.

Immediately contact your card issuer to report the loss. Most issuers have a 24/7 helpline. Monitor your account for unauthorized transactions and request a card replacement.

Use strong, unique passwords for online banking, enable transaction alerts, never share your card details, and regularly review your statements for suspicious activity.

A credit score is a number that represents your creditworthiness. Higher scores can help you get better loan terms. Pay bills on time, keep balances low, and avoid unnecessary credit inquiries to maintain a good score.
