How to Maintain a Good Credit Score

A good credit score opens doors to better financial opportunities. Here are the essentials for keeping your score healthy and strong:

Pay On Time

Payment history is the biggest factor in your score.

Keep Balances Low

Use less than 30% of your available credit.

Limit New Accounts

Apply for new credit only when needed.

Best Practices for a Great Score

  • Pay all bills on time, every time.
  • Keep your credit utilization low (under 30%).
  • Check your credit report regularly for errors or fraud.
  • Maintain a mix of credit types if possible.
Tip: A good credit score can help you qualify for lower interest rates and better rewards cards!
